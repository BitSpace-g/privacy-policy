Sending a support request

In the game, open Settings → Contact → Email Support. The draft includes your support ID, app version, platform, device model, and locale. Review the message and add what happened before sending it. Copy Support Info copies the same information and the support email address if you prefer another email app.

For a purchase issue, include your App Store or Google Play order ID if available. Do not send account passwords, verification codes, or payment-card details.

Common issues

Try closing and reopening the game, checking your connection, and installing the latest update. Before deleting or reinstalling the game to resolve a problem with tickets or purchases, contact us: the current anonymous wallet is not automatically linked to your leaderboard account.

Delete your game account or request data deletion

In the game, open Settings → Privacy Settings → Delete Game Account to request deletion of your anonymous game account and its associated records.

You can also email an account or data deletion request, including if you no longer have the app. Tell us whether you want the entire game account deleted or have a request about specific data. Include the game account ID or deletion receipt if you already have one. For purchase-related records, an App Store or Google Play order ID may help us locate the records. We may ask for information to identify the relevant account and verify your request; do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment-card details.

Account deletion removes the anonymous authentication identity, wallet, and associated reward and revive history. Paid and advertising tickets are lost and deletion does not issue a purchase refund. We retain minimal transaction hashes and store/product/deletion/refund status to prevent consumed purchases from being redeemed again. Completed server deletion receipts and temporary deletion markers expire after seven days. Apple and Google manage their own store and leaderboard records. See the privacy policy’s deletion and retention details.
